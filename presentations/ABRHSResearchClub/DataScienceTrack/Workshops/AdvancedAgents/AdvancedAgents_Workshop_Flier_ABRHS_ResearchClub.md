# ABRHS Research Club Data Science Workshop

**Workshop Title:** Advanced AI Agents Workshop

**Workshop Date:** [Day of the week, Month Day, Year]

Welcome to the "Advanced AI Agents Workshop", scheduled to take place on [Month Day, Year], within the Data Science track of the ABRHS Research Club.

**Workshop Objective:** The next step after Intro to AI Agents. Real agents have many tools, and part of their job is picking the right one. Participants will give the simplest agent a second tool and watch it decide which tool to use at each step, in about 30 lines of plain Python. No AI model, API key or installs needed.

**Workshop Outline:**

**A Harder Goal:**

* The new goal: a city that's warm and dry
* Why one tool isn't enough anymore

**The Agent:**

* Two tools, two sets of notes, one brain that picks the tool
* Watch it skip the checks it doesn't need

**Try It Yourself:**

* Change the weather and watch the agent's choices change
* Challenge: add a third tool

**Tools in Real AI Agents:**

* How a language model learns about its tools and picks one

**Prerequisites:**

* Intro to AI Agents (this workshop builds on its agent)
* Basic Python helpful but not required
* Nothing to install and no API key needed: everything runs in Google Colab
